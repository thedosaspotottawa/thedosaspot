# Design System: The Dosa Spot
**Project ID:** 14779053504747380282

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
The Dosa Spot embodies a **Modern South Indian Heritage** aesthetic. It blends traditional, earthy tones with a clean, high-contrast digital interface. The atmosphere is **Artisanal**, **Warm**, and **Inviting**, designed to evoke the sensory experience of a traditional Tamil kitchen while maintaining the sleekness of a premium tech-enabled restaurant.

## 2. Color Palette & Roles
The system uses a curated palette inspired by South Indian spices and flora.

*   **Deep Leaf Green (#16291d):** The primary brand color. Used for core brand identity, headings, and primary navigation icons. It represents fresh curry leaves and the vibrancy of South Indian vegetation.
*   **Earthy Coffee Brown (#2d1e12):** The secondary color. Used for high-impact text and secondary UI elements. It evokes the richness of roasted filter coffee and traditional spices.
*   **Golden Saffron (#d4a017):** The primary accent color. Used for highlights, decorative icons, and call-to-action elements. It symbolizes turmeric, ghee, and the premium quality of the ingredients.
*   **Vibrant Spice Orange (#ec6d13):** A secondary accent used for micro-details, background glows, and status indicators.
*   **Pure Alabaster (#ffffff):** The primary background color for the Light theme, ensuring a clean, modern, and readable canvas.
*   **Dark Onyx (#0f0a06):** The primary background color for the Dark theme, providing a deep, sophisticated night-mode experience.

## 3. Typography Rules
The typography system uses a mix of playful script and modern sans-serif fonts to balance heritage with usability.

*   **Headlines (Whimsical):** Uses **"Twinkle Star"** (#TwinkleStar). This script-like display font is used for H1 and H2 headers to inject personality and a handmade, welcoming feel.

## 4. Component Stylings
*   **Buttons:** Generally feature **subtly rounded corners** (`rounded-lg`) with bold typography. Primary buttons use the Deep Leaf Green (#16291d) or Golden Saffron (#d4a017) backgrounds with high-contrast text.
*   **Cards & Containers:** Utilize **gently curved edges** (`rounded-xl`) and **deep, elegant shadows** (`shadow-xl`) to create a sense of elevation and depth.
*   **Banners & CTAs:** Feature **generously rounded corners** (`rounded-xl`) and often utilize **Glassmorphism** (semi-transparent backgrounds with blur effects) to create a premium, layered feel.
*   **Image Grids:** Images are often presented in a **layered collage** style with varied aspect ratios and subtle offsets to emphasize the "handcrafted" nature of the brand.

## 5. Layout Principles
*   **Whitespace Strategy:** Generous "breathing room" between major sections (e.g., `py-12`) keeps the design feeling airy and high-end.
*   **Grid Alignment:** Uses a structured grid system for menu items and features, while story sections break the grid slightly to feel more organic.
*   **Micro-Animations:** Employs **Framer Motion** for smooth page transitions and entry animations (opacity fades and subtle Y-axis translations) to make the interface feel alive.
